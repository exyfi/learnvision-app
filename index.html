LearnVision privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

LearnVision does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data.

  • Photos you pick for the coach or the culling exercise stay on your iPhone. The app reads them on the device and sends them nowhere.
  • The back camera runs only while the live light meter or the in-app capture is open. The app keeps no frames from the meter.
  • Lesson progress, recipes you save and your reminder time stay on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone.
  • The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs.

Questions: see the support page.
